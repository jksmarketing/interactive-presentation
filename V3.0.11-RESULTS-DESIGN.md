# V3.0.11 — Presentation-native TV results

Research references: Mentimeter Quiz Competition (https://help.mentimeter.com/en/articles/410463-how-to-create-a-quiz-competition), Slido quiz (https://community.slido.com/interactive-poll-types-210/create-and-run-a-quiz-538), AhaSlides leaderboard and Kahoot themes (https://kahoot.com/themes/). The new layout uses a separate brand header and horizontal score bars rather than a floating results card.

- Applies to both competition reveals and published results, including automatic group result distributions, in the TV renderer, live previews and editor results previews.
- Uses the chosen slide text colour and title font; a light or dark translucent wash helps legibility. Uses text effects on titles and names. No hard-coded event name.
- Result-only logo positions are reserved in the top-left or top-right based on the saved horizontal position. Logo width is capped at 16% and height at 15% to prevent overlaps. The original saved placement and size remain unchanged on normal slides. Result previews do not enable free logo dragging because results have a constrained branding slot.
- TV rankings omit small vote-count labels for clarity; mobile rankings and private score views retain counts. Individual group distributions still show vote counts. Existing phone rendering is retained.
- Eight groups per page, larger competitions rotate pages as before. No new backend changes.

Verification: 12 Node regression suites and 43 Chromium results layout checks passed. Tests cover full HD and HD, participant view, controller preview, long titles, paging, and logo bounding-box separation for both header sides. Chromium uses a mocked API; actual Cloudflare deployment and physical TVs were not tested. Test screenshot uses synthetic artwork and logo, not the user's saved deck.
